# Early reward review

Status: the user approved quest-book-only trophies. Material rewards may deliberately help beginners; no reward amounts have changed.

## Current native course

- Welcome: 5 XP.
- Controls: 2 Apples; optional sprint-jump practice: 2 Apples and 5 XP.
- Shelter: 3 White Wool, explicitly used by the Bed lesson.
- Safe home: 3 Cobblestone, 5 XP, 2 Apples, then 3 Bread and 1 Lantern.

These rewards are small, but not progression-neutral. Wool replaces gathering the Bed materials; Cobblestone replaces mining; food provides a survival buffer; the Lantern supplies usable lighting. Increasing XP also grants a gameplay resource rather than a purely cosmetic reward.

## Approved direction

Personal chapter badges and illustrated trophy cards now appear inside the quest book through the title-bar cup button. Themes: orientation, movement practice, first safe sleep, and a secure home. Eligibility comes from saved quest completions. Their value is cosmetic: no stat bonuses, crafting ingredients, consumable currency or physical trophy items. The collection remains under in-game review.

The user explicitly approved helpful early rewards: three Wool enable sleeping through the dangerous first nights, and Cobblestone compensates the learner's effort. Preserve this instructional safety bridge instead of applying a strict no-acceleration policy. Any future rebalance must avoid skipping lessons and preserve existing progress and reward journals.
